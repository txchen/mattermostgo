# Changelog

## 0.5.3

- "Check for Updates…" is in the Mattermost Go menu of the Mac menu bar, after About, as in other Mac apps. It stays in the account menu too.

## 0.5.2

- The Mac app updates itself too: it checks for a new version once a day, shows what is new, and installs it when you agree; the update takes over when you quit the app. "Check for Updates…" is in the account menu. Homebrew leaves the app to update itself.

## 0.5.1

- Linux and Windows update themselves: the app checks for a new version in the background, shows what is new, and installs it when you agree. "Check for Updates…" is in the account menu. The Mac app keeps updating with Homebrew, and the Debian and RPM packages with your package manager.
- Custom emoji of your server show in the text of messages, and complete as you type `:`.
- A link to a message of your server shows that message, with a way to it.
- Compact messages put the time, the name and the text on one line, as in the official app.

## 0.5.0

- Builds for Linux (Debian and RPM packages, and a tarball) and Windows (an installer), for x86-64 and ARM64, as a preview.

## 0.4.0

- Buttons, menus and dialogs from bots and integrations.
- Jump to a message from search, links and permalinks.
- Edit webhooks, slash commands and bot accounts.
- A message that arrived while a channel opened no longer goes missing.

## 0.3.0

- Search, recent mentions, saved and pinned messages, and the Threads view.
- Settings for notifications, display with soft dark themes, the sidebar and security.
- A full emoji picker, slash command completion, file previews, group messages, and windows of their own for channels and threads.

## 0.2.1

- The account menu: status, Do Not Disturb, custom status, profile.
- A click anywhere on a message opens its thread.

## 0.2.0

- Channel, category and member management, integrations, and link previews.

## 0.1.1

- Collapsed Reply Threads, as the official app shows them.

## 0.1.0

- The first version: servers, login with MFA, channels, direct messages, threads, and real-time updates.
